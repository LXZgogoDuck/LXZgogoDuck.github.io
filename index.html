I teach robots to learn from their mistakes, and I make plenty of my own.

email cv github linkedin blog

I am a first-year M.S. student in Electrical Engineering at Stanford University, advised by Chelsea Finn. I am interested in principled ways for robots to turn real-world experience into reliable improvement, and in scaling reinforcement learning toward general, transferable robot capabilities.

Before Stanford, I was a research intern at the AgiBot Embodied Research Center and at Microsoft Research Asia (blog highlight), and a research assistant at the Oxford Robotics Institute with Shimon Whiteson. I received my B.S. in Data Science from The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen (ranked 1/315), including a visiting year in Mathematics and Computer Science at the University of Oxford.

I am always happy to chat! Feel free to reach out by email!
